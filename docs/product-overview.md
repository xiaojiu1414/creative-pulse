# Product overview

## From content to revision decisions

Creative Pulse supports pre-publication creative review for brand teams,
creators, and agencies. It connects three questions: what does the creative
communicate, what evidence supports that reading, and what should the team change?

## Format-specific evaluation

Video reports combine a creative score with dimension-level judgments and
rule-level findings. Image-text reports use Claim, Persuasion, and Action
assessments. The two formats retain their own evaluation frameworks rather than
sharing a single scoring scale.

Reports connect findings to source frames, time positions, images, and text.
Reviewers can inspect the original material alongside the interpretation and
revision recommendations.

## Comparison with pulse

Reviewers select creatives from the library and open a pulse conversation.
Answers, citations, and follow-up questions stay connected to the selected
material, supporting comparison and specific revision discussions.

For example:

> Which creative communicates the product benefit most clearly, and why?
> Cite the supporting evidence.

## Campaign content exploration

The campaign view connects semantic themes, term associations, and group
patterns to the underlying creatives, alongside the review of individual assets.

## Interpretation and review

Model estimates and judgments remain distinguishable from measured content
signals. Creative review supports human decisions about revision and publication;
it does not establish observed advertising outcomes.
